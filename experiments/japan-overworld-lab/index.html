Historical reference

Atlas sources and interpretation

This is a fantasy-RPG demonstration with a modern geographic foundation, not an original 1638 map or a precise reconstruction of seventeenth-century roads, province boundaries, or settlements.

  • The coastline derives from the public-domain Natural Earth Japan country polygon (simplified for this map). Minor islands and relief symbols are illustrative.
  • The aesthetic takes inspiration from the national Kan'ei-era map of Japan generally dated to around 1638. National Diet Library original and NDL map history.
  • Maps of this period typically omitted Ezo (modern Hokkaido). Here it remains visible to preserve geographic recognition, and is styled separately in Atlas mode. University of Zurich map history.
  • Kyoto regional roads and the illustrated Dojo building are fictional map-game elements. Do not use this interface as a historical travel map.
