How to read this atlas

Evidence before interpretation.

Source-attested means an exact string appears at known V4 character coordinates. It does not prove word boundaries, senses, or grammatical functions.

Reading guides, roles, grammar explanations, and modern relevance are labeled study or editorial interpretations. Shirane and Rodrigues guide analysis but do not independently verify an individual token.
