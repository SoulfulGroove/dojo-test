How this rep works · scoring and support

Reconstruction trains recognition and chart organization; it is not the same as unaided recall. In the second rep, see a kana and type its sound without choices. An accurate first-try recall earns 10 session XP, a corrected response earns 2, and a revealed answer earns 0. Rebuilding a placement earns 2 only on the first attempt. XP is feedback for this run, not a mastery certification. No unreliable audio is presented.
