Experiment v1.9 · Gateway & Passage Systems

Transitions with meaning.

Five passage grammars test whether the Dojo can communicate why a transition is happening—not merely animate between screens.

Torii Gate Chapter Gateway

Semantic role: crossing from ordinary space into focused study.

Use for chapter entry, Dojo entry, or a deliberate change of cognitive mode.

Shoji Panel Passage

Semantic role: moving between neighboring rooms or closely related contexts.

Use for study → notes, reading → apparatus, or adjacent tools inside the same world.

Sumi-e Ink Wash Wipe

Semantic role: transformation—one state becomes another.

Unformed thought
Form revealed
Interpretation, synthesis, perspective shift
Use when content changes meaning or representation, not merely location.

Joinery Lock Transition

Semantic role: synthesis, fit, completion, or verified connection.

合
Use when two concepts, answers, components, or stages successfully resolve into one structure.

Iaijutsu Quick-Draw Unlock

Semantic role: decisive release of something earned, chosen, or gated.

CHAPTER UNLOCKED
one action · one decisive state change
Use sparingly for mastery gates, major unlocks, or decisive commitments.
